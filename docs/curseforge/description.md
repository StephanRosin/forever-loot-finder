# Forever Loot Finder

A gear finder for **WoW: Forever**. Pick a class, a role and a slot, and the list shows every dungeon and raid item that suits them, with the boss and instance it drops from. English, Deutsch.

## Requirements
- [AtlasLoot Classic Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/atlasloot-forever)
- [DungeonJournal](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/dungeonjournal)

Install both. The loot tables come from AtlasLoot Classic Forever.

## Classes and roles
- Each class has its own roles: Priest **Healer** and **Caster**; Paladin **Healer**, **Tank** and **Melee**; Druid **Healer**, **Caster**, **Tank** and **Melee**; and so on.
- Items are filtered by their stats: an item must have a stat the role wants and none the role excludes. Casters never see attack power or defense items, tanks never see spell power or mana regen.
- Spell damage items count for healers too, since they carry the same healing. Healing items do not count for casters.

## Filters
- **Wearable now**: only items for your level and the armour types you have learned.
- **Best armour type only**: body armour of your class's heaviest type only (mail or plate from level 40 on); cloaks, jewellery and trinkets always count.
- **Dungeons** and **raids**, each on or off.
- A **name search**.
- All 15 **equipment slots** as paper-doll icons.
- **Sort** by item level, quality or name.

## From the character window
- A small **search button** on every equipment slot opens the list for that slot and compares it with what you wear:
  - **Upgrades**: more points for your role and no less of its main stat.
  - **Alternatives**: about as good, or better in total but with less of the main stat (less healing but more spirit, for example). Items whose effects are not plain stats, like trinkets with a Use effect, are listed here too.
- The button's tooltip tells how many upgrades and alternatives there are.
- Size, anchor point and X/Y offset of the buttons can be set in the options.

## The list
- Each row shows the item level, the value that matters for the role and an **Upgrade** or **Alternative** mark.
- **Click** a row to see all its drop locations: boss and instance, with the drop chance when it is known.
- **Shift-click** links the item in chat, **ctrl-click** tries it on, hovering shows the item tooltip.

## Where to find it
The minimap button, `/flf`, or Blizzard's addon compartment.

## Themes
Styled like the other Forever addons, with all Forever themes and their colours. With the Forever Themes addon installed, it follows the central theme.

## Data
- The items and drop locations come from the dungeon and raid tables of **AtlasLoot Classic Forever**, read while you play. Nothing is copied from it.
- New Forever items that the server has not shown to anyone yet appear without stats.

## Commands
`/flf`
